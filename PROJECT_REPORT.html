1. Introduction & Background

The global tourism landscape in Sri Lanka has shifted toward luxury, authentic experiential retreats where travelers seek seamless digital convenience combined with traditional cultural hospitality. Heritage Coastal Villa is an artisan sanctuary nestled along the Mirissa coastline that blends native Ceylon teak architecture with modern coastal living.

Prior to this system, small-to-medium luxury villas managed reservations and guest inquiries through disjointed phone calls, manual email exchanges, or high-commission Third-Party Online Travel Agencies (OTAs). This resulted in high booking overhead, communication delays, missed revenue on experiential add-ons, and vulnerability to double-booking errors during peak coastal tourist seasons.

2. Project Objectives

The objective of this web application was to engineer a self-contained, high-performance, and visually captivating direct-booking platform. Key objectives include:

  • Direct Guest Empowerment: Provide prospective travelers with a real-time reservation workflow, dual currency conversion (USD / LKR), automated long-stay incentives (5% & 10% discounts), and transparent taxation (8% VAT).
  • Experiential Tourism Integration: Enable guests to bundle verified local experiences (Ayurvedic wellness therapies, marine dolphin/whale safaris, in-villa floating breakfasts, cultural cooking classes) directly with room reservations.
  • Complimentary Amenities Transparency: Highlight zero-cost guest privileges (unlimited infinity pool access, in-suite kitchenette, front beach access, 100Mbps WiFi) both dynamically and in administrative controls.
  • Policy Compliance & Orderliness: Enforce mandatory agreement to Villa Policies & House Rules (quiet hours, no outside food, pool safety, deposit rules) prior to booking confirmation.
  • Centralized Back-Office Management: Provide villa administrators with a secured portal to monitor revenue, review pending booking requests, manage suite rates, and maintain the experience catalog.

6. Explanation of Core Functions

6.1 Real-Time Stay & Currency Calculation (`book.php`)

The reservation form features an event-driven JavaScript computational engine paired with server-side validation in PHP (`process_booking.php`). When check-in/out dates or add-ons are toggled:

  • Duration Calculus: Computes $Nights = \text{Date}_{out} - \text{Date}_{in}$. Dates prior to current day or check-out $\le$ check-in are rejected.
  • Tiered Long-Stay Discount Engine: $$\text{Discount} = \begin{cases} 0.10 \times \text{Room Subtotal}, & \text{if } Nights \ge 3 \\ 0.05 \times \text{Room Subtotal}, & \text{if } Nights = 2 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
  • Dual-Currency Switcher: Converts all calculations using a defined constant $1\text{ USD} = 320\text{ LKR}$. Guests can switch displays between primary USD and converted LKR seamlessly.
  • Mandatory 8% VAT: Government tax is computed as $\text{VAT} = 0.08 \times (\text{Net Room Total} + \text{Addons Total})$.

6.2 Strict Double-Booking Concurrency Control (`process_booking.php`)

To prevent race conditions where two simultaneous visitors attempt to reserve the same suite on overlapping dates, transactions are executed with pessimistic row-locking:

SELECT * FROM rooms WHERE id = :id AND status = 'available' FOR UPDATE;
SELECT id FROM bookings WHERE room_id = :room_id AND booking_status IN ('pending', 'confirmed') AND NOT (check_out <= :new_in OR check_in >= :new_out) LIMIT 1;

If an overlap is detected, the transaction is rolled back with an immediate descriptive notification, preserving data consistency.

6.3 Mandatory House Rules & Policies Gate

Before order confirmation, guests are presented with the compiled Villa Policies & House Rules (2:00 PM Check-In, 11:00 AM Check-Out, No Outside Food/Beverages, Strictly No Pets, Indoor Smoking Prohibition, and Pool Safety Disclosures). A mandatory checkbox is validated both client-side and server-side to guarantee legal and operational consensus.

6.4 Dynamic Free Facilities & Experience CRUD

Administrators can configure both paid experiences and complimentary facilities ($0.00) through admin/services.php. An intelligent image resolver links localized photo assets from assets/images/ to ensure zero broken image states.

8. Challenges Faced & Lessons Learnt

8.1 Challenges Encountered

  • Database Schema Constraints with New Categories: Adding the free_facility category initially encountered strict MySQL ENUM constraints, causing blank category entries until an automated schema migration script was executed.
  • Sub-Pixel Card Alignment: Dynamic multi-line titles in experience cards caused uneven button heights on varying viewport sizes. Resolved by enforcing CSS Flexbox column alignment with minimum header heights and margin-top: auto footers.
  • Dual-Currency Real-Time Sync: Managing price states across JavaScript DOM calculations while maintaining exact financial accuracy with PHP server calculations required uniform rounding rules and centralized exchange constants.

8.2 Lessons Learnt

  • Defensive Database Programming: Relying on database transactions (`FOR UPDATE`) is essential for hospitality platforms to prevent race conditions that simple frontend checks cannot stop.
  • User-Centric Policy UX: Presenting house rules within a tidy, scrollable modal/box during checkout dramatically improves compliance without disrupting user booking sentiment.
  • Modular Architecture: Decoupling database configuration, templates (header/footer), and computational logic allows effortless expansion of additional villa properties and service packages.
